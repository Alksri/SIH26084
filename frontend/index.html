Features

Everything a duty forecaster needs, on one screen

Built for the minutes that matter — when a Nor'wester is forming and people need to act.

⏱️

Arrival countdowns

Minute-precise ETAs for 80+ cities, airports and rural districts, with the hazards to expect and how long they last.

🛰️

Multi-source fusion

Radar mosaics, parallax-corrected satellite and lightning fused on one 2 km grid. Radar gaps are filled from satellite + lightning.

🌱

Storms caught early

Rapid cloud-top cooling flags new thunderstorms before radar sees them, and lightning jumps warn of severe weather 10–30 min ahead.

⚠️

Four severe hazards

Lightning density, hail probability, downburst gust speed and cloudburst (≥100 mm/h) risk — per 10 minutes out to 6 hours.

✨

Ask in plain language

“Show hail risk near Ranchi in 2 hours.” The built-in assistant answers from live data and drives the map for you.

📤

Share & export

Printable bulletins, CSV arrival lists, GeoJSON for QGIS/ArcGIS, map images and shareable links to an exact view.

How it works

From raw sensors to a warning in under a minute

Ingest

Radar, satellite and lightning streams arrive, get quality-checked and are monitored for delays and outages.

Fuse

Everything lands on one 2 km grid, time-aligned and corrected for satellite parallax.

Detect

Storm cells are tracked, new storms spotted, lightning jumps flagged.

Forecast

Storms are moved along their motion with growth and decay, with probabilities out to 6 h.

Alert

Countdowns, banners, notifications and bulletins reach the people in the path.

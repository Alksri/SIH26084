How accurate is the forecast?

Every forecast is checked against what the radar actually saw when that time arrived. We also compare it with a simple guess that the storm just stays where it is — beating that guess shows the motion forecast is adding real value.

How it works

Every few minutes, BUMBLEBLE runs the same five steps.

  1. 📡
    Collect

    Gather the latest radar scans, satellite images and lightning reports.

  2. 🧩
    Combine

    Merge them into one picture. Satellite and lightning fill gaps where radar can’t see.

  3. 🔍
    Detect

    Find each storm, measure its strength and spot new storms as they start to form.

  4. 🔮
    Forecast

    Track how storms are moving and project where they will be over the next 6 hours.

  5. 🔔
    Warn

    Work out when each town, farm area and airport will be hit — and how badly.

Technical details

Scores compare forecast storm areas (radar reflectivity ≥ 35 dBZ) with later observations. CSI = critical success index, POD = probability of detection, FAR = false alarm ratio, Persistence = CSI of a “no movement” forecast.

Guidance only — always follow official IMD warnings.
